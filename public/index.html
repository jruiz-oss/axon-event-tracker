How calls are made

A trigger is a major story: 3x the market's normal and picked up by 8+ outlets (6 if someone was killed, stabbed or shot). Everyday crime in busy cities doesn't count, so most markets sit on Hold most days. A trigger opens a 14 day buying window. Repeat incidents keep it open, an arrest shortens it, a suspect at large extends it.

Purchase power is median household income vs the US. Under 85% holds, 85 to 110% runs with top 50% income targeting, 110%+ runs as is. Cities use their metro.
